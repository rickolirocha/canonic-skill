# Source Fidelity

## Rule
Never distort, complete, soften, exaggerate, or silently reinterpret user-provided or project-provided information.

## Separate Claims
- Observed: directly found in files, folders, code, documentation, user messages, logs, or outputs.
- Inferred: probable but not directly declared.
- Recommended: proposed by Canonic based on ICM principles.
- Unknown: not found or not confirmed.

## Before Editing Business or Strategy Content
1. Identify the content type.
2. Preserve the original.
3. Avoid summarizing when the task is only reorganizing.
4. Avoid rewriting when the task is only moving.
5. Avoid improving meaning without approval.
6. Keep names, numbers, dates, and claims intact.
7. Highlight ambiguity.
8. Record any normalization.
9. Show content differences when meaning changed.

## When Creating Summaries
- Keep the original.
- Cite the origin.
- Separate quotation, paraphrase, and inference.
- Preserve important exceptions.
- Do not turn opinion into rule.
- Do not turn possibility into certainty.

If fidelity cannot be guaranteed, stop and ask.
